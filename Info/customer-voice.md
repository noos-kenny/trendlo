# Customer Voice — Trendlo

What actual customers say, in their own words. This is the least exploited data source in most audits: reviews, support tickets, return comments and surveys contain directly usable messaging language, the exact objections creative has to overcome, and triggers customers articulate themselves.

Always keep verbatim quotes as quotes. Never paraphrase a quote into a claim.

## Sources
Where the voice was collected from (Trustpilot, Google reviews, Prisjakt, on-site product reviews, support inbox, return data, NPS, surveys, social listening) — including volume and date range per source.
